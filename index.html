About Me

My name is Sam Hardy, and welcome to my website. The simplest way that I would describe myself is through three different adjectives: iterative, holistic and experimental.

Iterative, because of my openness to change, striving to always be a better version of myself.

Holistic, because I'm always thinking deeply about details, big and small, and how they work together to form something greater than the sum of its parts.

Experimental, because I'm not afraid to try things to gain a different perspective on an advantage or problem.

My Design Philosophy

My work ethic involves meticulously trying variations on themes until a dynamic and fluid approach arises. It's important to me to pay attention to all aspects of the functions of things and how they affect my work.

When solving design problems, I'm a big fan of finding novel solutions so that I can gain a greater appreciation for the strengths and weaknesses of different approaches.

Through this site, I hope to continue to iterate, think holistically and try new things as I continue to solve all sorts of design problems.
