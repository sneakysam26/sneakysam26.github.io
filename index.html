About Me

My name is Sam Hardy, and welcome to my website. If I had to describe myself in three words, they would be iterative, holistic and experimental.

Iterative, because of my commitment to continuous improvement. I am open to change, striving to always refine myself and grow.

Holistic, because I think about the finer details and how they work together to form something greater than the sum of its parts.

Experimental, because I am endlessly curious and not afraid to explore unconventional ideas. I enjoy testing several approaches to gain a different perspective on a problem.

My Design Philosophy

My design philosophy is based on exploration and continual improvement. I enjoy experimenting with different variations and possibilities until I arrive at an approach that aligns with my vision.

It is important to me to pay attention to all details and how they affect my work. I believe good design requires an understanding of the individual elements and the larger systems they exist within.

When solving design problems, I enjoy experimenting with a number of novel solutions and iterating on ideas so that I can gain a greater appreciation for the strengths and weaknesses of different approaches.

Through this website, I hope to showcase a willingness to iterate, think holistically and experiment with creative ideas as I continue to learn and solve a variety of design challenges.

My Process

My work develops through exploration, experimentation and refinement. I use an iterative process to explore different approaches, understand how individual decisions affect the larger project and develop the strongest solution I can.

01

Plan

I begin by defining the problem, gathering ideas and establishing an initial direction before moving into production.

02

Explore & Iterate

I explore different visual directions, compositions and techniques before committing to a single solution.

I compare variations, test ideas and learn from approaches that don't work as expected. Each iteration helps move the project towards a stronger solution.

03

Refine

Once a direction begins to work, I focus on the details and consider how each element contributes to the project as a whole.

04

Deliver

The final result is the outcome of that process: planning, experimentation, iteration and careful refinement working together.
